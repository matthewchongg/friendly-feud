📡 Wi-Fi & Network Diagnostics

Campus and enterprise Wi-Fi networks (such as PolyU Wi-Fi or eduroam) often block peer-to-peer WebSockets, UDP ports, or client-to-client connections. Test your connection below:

🛠️ How to Play If Wi-Fi Has a Blocker:

  1. 📱 Method 1 (Recommended): Mobile Hotspot
    Turn on Personal Hotspot on one phone. Connect the host laptop to it. Cellular data has zero campus firewall restrictions!
  2. 📶 Method 2: Contestants on 4G / 5G
    Contestants don't need Wi-Fi! Simply tell players to turn off Wi-Fi on their phones and use their mobile data (4G/5G) to open the buzzer.
  3. 💻 Method 3: Offline / Dual-Screen Mode (0% Internet)
    Connect your laptop to the projector with HDMI (Press Win + P → Extend). Drag the Game Board to the projector screen and keep Admin on your laptop. Everything syncs locally with zero internet needed!
