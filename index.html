Rules and Mechanics Guide

1. The Face-Off

One representative from each competing group approaches the podium for the initial question. The faster player to hit the buzzer answers. Revealing the #1 Answer gives immediate board control. If lower, the 2nd player guesses: higher rank gains control. If neither guesses on the board, control defaults to the first player's team.

2. Playing the Board

Members of the controlling team take individual turns guessing remaining answers. No consultation or hints allowed! Each player has a 20-second time limit once called upon.

3. Three Strikes Rule

Every incorrect guess results in one Strike (X). Upon accumulating Three Strikes, the controlling team immediately loses control and the opposing team gets an opportunity to steal!

4. The Steal & Winning

The opposing team gets 15 seconds of group conferral. The designated captain presents the answer: if on the board, the opposing team steals ALL accumulated round points! Points are tallied across all rounds to crown the champion.
