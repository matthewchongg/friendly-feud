Rules & Mechanics

Complete official guide to playing and supervising live Family Feud matches.

Phase 1

⚡ The Face-Off Podiums

  • Podium Representatives: One appointed player from each competing team approaches the podium for the initial question. Only these two players have active buzzers during the face-off.
  • The Faster Player: The first player to hit their buzzer gets the first opportunity to answer the survey question.
  • #1 Answer Direct Win: If the buzzer winner reveals the #1 ranked survey answer, their team immediately gains control of the board without the opponent getting a guess.
  • Higher Rank Advantage: If the first answer is on the board but is not #1, the opposing player gets a turn to guess. Whichever player guessed the higher-ranked answer gains control of the board for their team.
  • First Player Default: If neither player's guess appears on the board, control defaults to the first player's team line.
💡 Supervision Tip: Use the Face-Off wizard on the Admin screen to arm buzzers and record whether the answer was #1, lower rank, or a miss.
Phase 2

🎯 Playing the Board

  • Turn-Based Guessing: Members of the controlling team take individual turns in order guessing remaining survey answers on the board.
  • No Consultation: Teammates are strictly forbidden from conferring, whispering, or gesturing during standard board play. Each guess must come solely from the player whose turn it is.
  • 20-Second Time Limit: Each player has exactly 20 seconds to state their answer once called upon by the host.
  • Clearing the Board: If the controlling team reveals all survey answers before accumulating three strikes, they bank and keep all points for that round.
⏱️ Timer Enforcement: Trigger the built-in 20-second countdown on the Admin panel to keep the game energetic and prevent stalling.
Phase 3

❌ The Three Strikes Rule

  • Strike (X): Every incorrect guess or time-out results in one Strike on the board accompanied by the classic buzzer sound.
  • Accumulating 3 Strikes: Upon accumulating three strikes (XXX), the controlling team immediately loses control of the board.
  • Opportunity to Steal: The board freezes and the round transitions to the Steal phase.
Phase 4

🚨 The Steal

  • 15 Seconds Group Conferral: The opposing team is granted 15 seconds of open discussion to confer and agree on a single final answer.
  • Captain Speaks: The designated team captain presents the final agreed answer to the host.
  • Successful Steal: If the answer appears on the board, the opposing team steals all accumulated points in the round bank!
  • Unsuccessful Steal: If the answer is not on the board, the original controlling team retains all points in the round bank.
🔔 Sound Effect: The Steal phase features a distinct gong and an urgent 15-second countdown timer.
Phase 5

🏆 Scoring & Winning the Match

  • Round Multipliers: Points can be multiplied in later rounds (e.g. Round 1: 1X, Round 2: 2X, Round 3+: 3X Triple Points).
  • Dynamic Custom Rounds: The match can be configured for any custom number of rounds (default: 3 rounds).
  • Grand Winner: Points are tallied across all rounds. The team with the highest cumulative total is declared the Grand Winner with full screen confetti and victory fanfare!
